Questions

The things people ask first.

What if the creator doesn't deliver?

Your money is held, not sent. If the agreed content never goes live, it comes back to you. We approve the content before it's posted, so you see it first.

How is this different from just DMing a creator myself?

You can, and some people do. What you don't get is their real rate rather than the stranger price, a record of whether they actually deliver, an escrow that protects you both, paperwork for your books, or a report at the end. DMing gets you a conversation. This gets you a campaign.

How do you know their followers are near my shop?

Creators connect their Instagram account to Spotly, so their follower numbers and audience-city breakdown come from Instagram directly rather than from a screenshot. We combine that with where they live, how far they travel, and where they actually shoot.

I'm a creator with 4,000 followers. Am I too small?

No — small local accounts are what most of our businesses want. A 4,000-follower account where the followers live in Kandivali is more useful to a Kandivali gym than a 40,000-follower account spread across India.

Do you work outside these areas?

Not yet, honestly. We'd rather be genuinely useful in three pincodes than useless across the city. Message us anyway — if there's demand in your area, it moves up the list.
